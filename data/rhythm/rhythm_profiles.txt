# Formant rhythm profiles (#144), version 1

# Target bands come from Grabe and Low (2002); see PROVENANCE.md. Each rhythm
# line is:
#   rhythm NAME CLASS V-NPVI-MIN V-NPVI-MAX I-RPVI-MIN I-RPVI-MAX STRENGTH MIN-FACTOR MAX-FACTOR
# nPVI is dimensionless (x100); rPVI is in milliseconds. Strength is the
# per-iteration fraction of the gap closed, and the factors bound how far any
# individual phoneme duration may move from its authored value.
